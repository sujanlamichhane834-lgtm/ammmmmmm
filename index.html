FOR DIKSHYA
A little piece of my heart
A letter I couldn't say out loud

For Dikshya.

There are things I have felt, things I have feared, and things I have never quite known how to put into words.
Take your time. This is from my heart.
01 — The beginning

I wasn't looking for love.

I didn't come talking to you with the intention of loving you, Dikshya. Honestly, I was already done with the word love.

I wasn't searching for someone. I wasn't planning to fall for anyone. I thought that part of my life was already behind me.

And then, somewhere between our conversations and all the little moments, something changed. I couldn't tell you the exact day or the exact moment. I just know that one day you had become someone I cared about in a way I hadn't planned.

One thing that truly stayed with me was your transparency. You kept things clear even when I never asked you to explain. You didn't have to make a big announcement about it. It was simply the way you were, and it meant more to me than you probably know.

"I wasn't searching for love. Somehow, I found you."
02 — The things I struggle to say

My silence isn't a lack of feeling.

कहिलेकाहीँ मनमा धेरै कुरा हुन्छन्, तर कसरी भन्ने भन्ने नै थाहा हुँदैन।

Sometimes you tell me that you don't feel I'm completely clear about you, or that you wonder whether I genuinely want you.

I wish I could explain this better: my hesitation isn't because I don't want you. A lot of it comes from not knowing how to express what is happening inside me. I can feel something deeply and still struggle to find the right words.

I don't want you to have to guess. I want to learn to communicate better, because you deserve clarity, not confusion.

03 — The man I'm still becoming

My biggest fear is not having everything figured out.

I am pursuing Computer Science. I believe in what I'm learning, but I also see how quickly the world is changing.

The job market is competitive. Technology keeps changing. AI and automation are reshaping work in almost every field. Sometimes I look at all of it and genuinely wonder what the future of the path I'm pursuing will look like.

I know my education matters. I know I have dedication. I am consistent, and I want to evolve. But I also know that becoming established takes time.

Somewhere in my mind, I feel that a settled man can offer a kind of security that a man who is still building himself cannot offer immediately. And that thought scares me. I don't want you to feel that you have to wait for me to become someone I haven't become yet.

But please understand the difference: I need time to build the life I dream of. I do not need to wait to care for you, respect you, make efforts, or love you.

"I don't need time to love you. I need time to build the life I dream of sharing."
04 — Why I sometimes say you deserve better

Sometimes I doubt myself.

When I say I hope you find the kind of person you deserve, please don't hear it as me asking you to leave. It comes from a place of insecurity that I don't always know how to explain.

I know there are people who might be more settled than me, more confident, more handsome, or more ready to offer a comfortable life from the very beginning. Someone else might have everything figured out already. Sometimes I struggle to look at myself and confidently say, "That person is me."

But I have dreams too. I dream of giving you a garden, a beautiful home, a car, comfort, experiences, and the little luxuries that make life beautiful. I want to build those things with hard work and patience. I am not promising that everything will appear tomorrow. I am telling you what I want to work towards.

05 — What I can give today

I don't want to postpone loving you.

🎁

Small efforts

I can surprise you, give little gifts, remember details, make time, listen, and find thoughtful ways to make your ordinary days brighter.

🌱

Growing every day

I don't know how to sing or dance. I don't have every talent. But I can learn, improve, create, and keep trying to become better.

I may not be exceptionally talented. Apart from being a little smart and making things like this website, I sometimes feel I don't have much to show. But I can show effort. I can be thoughtful. I can make you feel appreciated.

I want to be praised by you, yes. I want more time with you. I want to feel important in your life. I want to be someone you choose, and someone who makes you feel chosen too.

06 — The honest, imperfect part

Yes, I get jealous.

I want to be honest instead of pretending I never feel it. I sometimes hate hearing about another boy or your ex. Sometimes, when you mention a particular background, design, website, or creative thing you want, I feel a little ache because those are things I believe I could make for you — and I want to be the person who gets to do that.

I know jealousy is my feeling to handle. It is not a rule you have to live by, and it should never become a reason to limit your freedom. I don't want to control who you talk to or what you like. I want to feel secure, special, and close to you.

Sometimes I hold back my best efforts because I wonder whether you are happy with me. I am trying to understand that I should communicate that fear instead of letting it speak for me.

07 — A little family memory

Your name has reached my home, too.

Asti, when your grandmother — my thulo momi — visited our home, my mother asked her, "Hajur, naatinīharu kasto chhan?"

She asked because she knows I like you. And, by the way, my whole family knows about you.

I felt happy about that. You aren't just someone whose name appears in my phone. You have become someone I talk about at home, someone whose mention brings a quiet happiness with it.

08 — Inside my heart

Six parts of one feeling.

These things may seem different, but they all come from one place: how much you have come to mean to me.

09 — What I want you to be able to trust

Not perfection. Intention, and action.

I cannot promise that I will never make you cry. I am human, and there may be misunderstandings or days when I fail to express myself well. But I can promise that I will take responsibility when I hurt you and make a genuine effort to repair what I have damaged.

I will never raise my hand against you. I will never cheat on you or betray your trust. Those are values I was taught, and values I choose for myself.

I want to respect you, listen to you, be honest with you, and keep learning how you need to be loved. I don't want to love you only when everything is easy. I want to work through the difficult days with patience and care.

♡ Respect
I will respect your choices, voice, and individuality.
♡ Loyalty
I will be faithful and honest with you.
♡ Effort
I will keep showing up in little and meaningful ways.
♡ Accountability
If I hurt you, I will listen, own it, and try to make it right.
"There won't always be highs. There will be ups and downs. I want to keep choosing us through both."
10 — Everything I couldn't say

My uncertainty about myself
is not uncertainty about you.

Dikshya, I don't know when I fell for you. I don't know how someone who was already done with the word love ended up having so much to say about one person.

I don't know how to express everything I feel. Sometimes I question myself. Sometimes I question my future. Sometimes I wonder whether I am enough for you.

But I don't want you to mistake my uncertainty about myself for uncertainty about wanting you.

I might still be evolving. I might still be building my career. I might still be figuring out what life has planned for me. But I don't want to wait until I become successful to start loving you properly.

I want to love you while I build my future. I want to celebrate your little achievements, surprise you, make you smile, and be there on your ordinary days. I want to work towards the garden, the house, the car, and all those beautiful things I dream of giving you.

But before all of that, I want to give you what I can give today: my efforts, my loyalty, my honesty, my respect, and my love.

I cannot promise a life without difficult days. I can promise that I want to learn, understand, and not walk away just because things stop being easy.

I wasn't searching for love. But somehow, I found you. And if there is one thing I genuinely want to get right in this evolving, confusing, uncertain life of mine, it's us.

One last thing, Dikshya

After everything I've said...

Can you trust me?

Not because I'm perfect. Not because I have everything figured out. But because I genuinely want to grow, love, understand you, and build something beautiful with you.

Will you give me the chance to show you that my feelings are real?

Your answer is yours. I wanted you to know what was in my heart.

Made with a heart that is still learning how to say everything. ♡ — Sujan
